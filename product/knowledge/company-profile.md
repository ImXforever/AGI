# Zenovix — AI & Digital Technology

Zenovix is a Dubai-based studio for AI and digital technology. It is a team of engineers, designers and AI specialists building the systems modern businesses run on. The studio works like a partner, not a vendor — close to the client's goals and obsessive about the details.

Tagline: "Intelligent systems for ambitious brands." Positioning: "Engineered in Dubai · Delivered worldwide" — a global network with the Gulf at its centre. From bilingual (English/Arabic) platforms for the Gulf to AI products for global markets, Zenovix turns ambition into shipped, measurable technology.

## Values
- Strategy first. We understand the business before we write a line of code.
- Craft in the details. Motion, performance, and polish are not optional.
- Built to last. Systems that scale and teams that stay with you after launch.

## Six service lines
01 Artificial Intelligence · 02 Cloud & ICT · 03 Web & Mobile · 04 Intelligent Automation · 05 Data & Analytics · 06 Animation & 3D. One partner for strategy, design, engineering, and the AI that ties it together.
