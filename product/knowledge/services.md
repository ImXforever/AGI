# Services catalogue

Every service is delivered as a scoped project. Prices, timelines and team size are confirmed in a formal proposal after a short discovery call — the assistant never estimates them.

## 01 Artificial Intelligence (code 101)
Custom models, computer vision, and conversational AI trained on your data and shipped production-ready. Typical work: assistants and chatbots on the client's own knowledge, document understanding and extraction, image and video analysis, forecasting and recommendation models, deployment and monitoring. Proposal inputs: use case, data available, channels and integrations, timeline.

## 02 Cloud & ICT (code 102)
Secure infrastructure, networks, and systems integration engineered for scale and reliability. Typical work: cloud architecture and migration, DevOps and CI/CD, observability, identity and security hardening, backup and recovery, integration between business systems. Proposal inputs: current environment, workloads, compliance needs, timeline.

## 03 Web & Mobile (code 103)
Fast, beautiful websites and apps — bilingual EN/AR ready — with motion and detail that convert. Typical work: corporate websites, landing pages, e-commerce, web applications, iOS and Android apps, design systems, performance optimisation, full right-to-left support. Proposal inputs: pages or screens, languages, integrations, timeline.

## 04 Intelligent Automation (code 104)
AI agents and workflow automation that remove friction so your team focuses on judgement. Typical work: customer-service and sales agents on WhatsApp, Telegram, e-mail and web; back-office and document workflows; human-in-the-loop approvals for sensitive actions; CRM / ERP / messaging integrations; monitoring and audit trail. Proposal inputs: processes to automate, channels, systems to integrate, timeline.

## 05 Data & Analytics (code 105)
Dashboards and pipelines that turn raw numbers into decisions — accurate and clear. Typical work: data warehousing and ETL, BI dashboards, KPI reporting, data quality and governance, analytics on operational data. Proposal inputs: data sources, KPIs and audiences, refresh frequency, timeline.

## 06 Animation & 3D (code 106)
Brand films, AI video, and interactive 3D that give your technology a story worth watching. Typical work: product and explainer animation, motion graphics, AI-generated video, 3D product visualisation, interactive web 3D. Proposal inputs: format and length, style references, languages, timeline.
