# How Zenovix works

## Process
1. Strategy first — a short discovery call to understand the business goal, users, languages and constraints before any code is written.
2. Proposal — scope, milestones, timeline and price in one document, confirmed by a manager. No prices are given in chat.
3. Design and engineering — craft in the details: motion, performance and polish are part of the definition of done.
4. Launch and beyond — built to last: maintenance, monitoring and iteration plans so the team stays with the client after go-live.

## Starting a project
Describe the goal in the chat (or press Quote), e-mail studio@zenovix.com, or use the contact form on zenovix.ae. The assistant collects the basics — service line, what you want to build and for whom, languages, integrations, timeline, company and contact — and a manager reviews it. First reply is usually the same business day.

## Ownership, privacy and payments
- Ownership and IP terms are defined in the proposal and contract; the default approach is that the client owns the delivered solution and its data.
- Enquiry details are used only to answer the enquiry and prepare a proposal; they are not shared with third parties.
- The assistant never takes payments, signs contracts, changes prices or deletes data — those always go to a human manager.
