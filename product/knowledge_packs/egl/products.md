# Products — Clean & Dirty Oils

EGL specialises in the safe storage and handling of both dirty and clean oils, moved through Fujairah under
strict HSE and quality controls.

## Dirty oils — Crude & Residual Fuel Oil
High-volume dirty oil handling with segregated tankage, spill-contained transfer points and continuous quality
monitoring.
- **Crude Oil** — bulk crude storage and trade support, handled under strict containment and safety protocol
  from receipt through discharge. (Bulk Storage · Marine Receipt · Quality Monitored)
- **Fuel Oil** — residual and marine fuel oil storage supporting bunkering demand across Fujairah's shipping
  lanes. (Bunker Grade · Segregated Tankage · HSE Handling)

## Clean oils — Diesel, Gasoline & Jet Fuel
Clean product lines stored and handled to tight quality tolerances, with full traceability from tank to transfer.
- **Diesel 10 PPM** — ultra-low sulphur diesel (max 10 ppm sulphur) stored under contamination-controlled
  conditions. (Ultra-Low Sulphur · Contamination Control)
- **Gasoline** — motor gasoline grades handled with vapour control and strict blending integrity.
  (Vapour Control · Blend Integrity)
- **Jet Fuel** — aviation-grade fuel storage held to elevated purity and documentation standards.
  (Aviation Grade · Strict Traceability)

## Same standard of care for every product
- Dedicated tankage to prevent cross-contamination
- TSA & TSR verified documentation on every movement
- HSE-compliant loading, discharge and transfer procedures
