# Services — Elian Global Logistics

Storage, movement, chartering, trade and compliance — coordinated as one operation, not six separate vendors.

## 01 Fujairah Terminal Storage
Modern tank storage for clean and dirty petroleum products at Fujairah.
- Segregated tankage for crude, fuel oil, diesel, gasoline and jet fuel
- Direct pipeline and marine access for fast turnaround
- Continuous quality and inventory monitoring
- UAE port and customs regulatory alignment
Quote inputs: product grade, volume, storage period/timeline.

## 02 Logistics & Bunkering
Integrated marine logistics and fuel supply for vessels in Fujairah and beyond.
- Coordinated bunker fuel delivery for vessels at anchorage or berth
- End-to-end shipment coordination and documentation
- HSE-compliant fuel handling at every transfer point
Quote inputs: vessel name/IMO, port and ETA, fuel grade, quantity (MT).

## 03 Ship Chartering
Access to Aframax, Suezmax and VLCC charters for global crude and refined product transport.
- Vessel sourcing across major tanker classes
- Route, laycan and freight coordination
- Charter party and cargo documentation support
Quote inputs: cargo type/quantity, load and discharge ports, laycan window.

## 04 Trade Solutions
End-to-end petroleum trade facilitation with financial and legal assurance.
- Buyer and seller matching for clean and dirty oil cargoes
- Structured trade support with financial safeguards
- Contract, LC and settlement coordination
Any contract, price or payment commitment requires manager approval.

## 05 TSA & TSR Document Verification
Authenticated validation of Tank Storage Agreements (TSA) and Tank Storage Receipts (TSR).
- Verification against terminal records
- Fraud prevention for storage and title documentation
- Clear audit trail for banks, traders and counterparties
Request inputs: document type, issuing terminal, document reference/date.

## 06 Consulting & Compliance Advisory
- Regulatory and HSE compliance guidance
- Market intelligence for storage and trade decisions
- Operational optimization across storage and logistics chains

## Pricing policy
No list prices are published. Every price is provided by formal quotation prepared and approved by the
commercial team. The assistant must never state or estimate a price.
