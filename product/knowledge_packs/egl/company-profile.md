# Elian Global Logistics Co. L.L.C (EGL)

**Tagline:** Petroleum Storage · Trading · Marine Logistics
**Website:** https://egl.co.ae (English) · https://egl.co.ae/ar/ (Arabic)

Elian Global Logistics is a UAE-registered oil and gas logistics company delivering secure petroleum storage,
trading support, marine logistics, bunkering, ship chartering and supply-chain services from Fujairah and Dubai.
It connects Fujairah — one of the world's busiest bunkering hubs — to international energy markets "with the
discipline of an operator and the reach of a trading house".
