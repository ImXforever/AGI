# Health, Safety, Environment & Compliance

Safety and regulatory compliance define how every operation is planned, from tank storage to vessel chartering.

## Why it matters
Petroleum storage and trade only work when every counterparty — terminal, trader, financier and vessel operator
— can trust the paperwork and the process behind it. EGL operations follow international HSE standards and
align with UAE port and customs regulations.

- International HSE-aligned operating procedures
- UAE port and customs regulatory alignment
- Transparent, verifiable documentation on every movement

## Four pillars
1. **Safety** — rigorous protocols across storage, transfer and marine operations to protect people and product
2. **Compliance** — operating procedures aligned to international standards and UAE regulatory requirements
3. **Environmental Responsibility** — spill prevention, containment planning and sustainable handling
4. **24/7 Operations** — continuous monitoring and support

## In practice
- Segregated, monitored storage: clean and dirty products stored separately with continuous monitoring
- Verified documentation: TSA and TSR verification protects every counterparty from fraudulent paperwork
- Controlled marine transfer: bunkering and cargo transfer follow strict procedure to prevent spillage

## Emergencies
Spills, leaks, fires or injuries are never handled by the assistant. Direct the customer to local emergency
services first, then to the 24/7 desk on +971 54 456 9977, and alert a manager immediately.
